# Watch-time rules (style-independent)

Apply to every script in `queue/`, whatever style is active. Based on our own reels and a frame-by-frame analysis (2026-11-13) of 11 narrator reference reels: bombilla (culturargentiina), football hen, fox photography, path to pope, cup noodles, football shirts, lobizón legend, Qatar final, horno de barro, "¿Qué hacés?" POV story, 24 h counter. Reference links: project memory `reference-reels`.

## 1. The hook (0–2 s) decides everything
- **One strong image + one claim, from frame 0.** Every reference opens on a single, instantly readable picture that already carries the headline (frame 0 = thumbnail): crossed-out bombilla "SIN BOMBILLA", a hen in a football shirt, the finished prize photo, the Pope next to the flag, a cup labelled "FIDEOS", a wall of 30 shirts, a scoreboard at minute 6'.
- **Hook types that worked:**
  1. *Surprising fact as a contradiction* – "Antes de la bombilla, el colador eran los dientes."
  2. *"¿Por qué…?" question with a strange detail* – "¿Por qué el presidente es padrino del séptimo hijo varón?"
  3. *Result first* – show the finished thing (prize photo, finished oven), then "así se hace".
  4. *Topical hook* – "Ahora que el Papa…", "La final de Qatar…".
  5. *"Qué hacés si…" dilemma in 2nd person* – puts the viewer inside the story.
- Spoken hook ≤ 10 words; the headline appears by 0.4 s; no greeting, no channel intro.
- The hook picture stays 3–6 s (it is not cut away early): the first beats are explained **on** that same picture with small overlays (X marks, labels, numbers).

## 2. Open loop by second 5–10
- Promise something that only comes later: hidden "?" cards to be revealed, "y la tercera es la más rara", a running clock (6' → 26' → 45' …), "uno por uno", a ladder of steps.
- **Visual counters keep people watching:** a clock, a score, a step number, an hour counter, a growing build. Use one when the topic allows (`number`, `timeline`, `list` visuals, numbered steps).

## 3. Body: one idea per picture, 2–4 s each
- New visual change every 2–4 s (a new shot, a reveal, a label, a zoom); a full scene change every 4–8 s.
- Visual grammar that repeats in almost all references: numbered cards/pins, red X for the wrong way, check for the right way, label pills pointing at parts, big numbers on a solid colour, a comparison side by side.
- **Escalation:** order points from known → surprising → most surprising. The strongest fact goes last before the payoff.
- One recurring **hero** (a mascot, the same object, the same character or set) gives continuity: the bombilla reel keeps one gourd for 18 s, the oven reel keeps one diorama for 118 s.

## 4. Re-hooks every 8–12 s
- A short pattern break: "Pero esperá…", a sudden close-up, a colour change, a sound hit, a question to "vos". Mark with `impact: true`.

## 5. Payoff (last 20 %)
- Deliver the promise with the strongest picture, then **land on an emotion or a joke** (pope reel ends on a laughing mascot "¡JAJA!", the oven on everyone eating, the cebador on "compañía").

## 6. Ending that creates comments and replays
- **Comment bait in the last 3 s:** a choice with 2–4 short options shown as buttons/pills ("¿Vos cuál usás?", "Comentá 1 o 2", "¿Cuál querés en la parte 2?"). The comments under the references show it works: people answer the options and suggest the next topic.
- **Series hook:** "Parte 1", "¿qué leyenda querés en la parte 2?" → followers come back.
- **Loop:** the last frame matches the first (hen → hen, shirts → shirts, gourd → gourd), so the replay is seamless and counts as extra watch time.
- "Seguinos para más" in the last sentence.

## 7. Captions, text, sound
- Small karaoke captions at the top, one highlighted word, readable on any background (box).
- On-screen headlines max 3 words; numbers big.
- The narrator never stops for more than ~0.6 s; music low under the voice; a soft hit on every reveal.
- Post caption repeats the hook, adds one value line and ends with the same question as the video (+ 5–8 hashtags). Credits for images/music when used.

## 8. Our format limits
- **Length: 35–45 s** (references run 58–118 s; we stay shorter for completion rate). With Charon: 12–14 segments (~100–110 words).
- Rioplatense, voseo, only Spanish on screen. 2–3 sensory/human moments per reel. No invented facts, no health claims.
- Vary `emo`, never the same three times in a row.

## Checklist for every script
1. Hook: one picture + one claim, frame 0 has the headline? Which hook type?
2. Open loop or visual counter by 5–10 s?
3. Points ordered by surprise, one idea per segment?
4. Re-hook every 8–12 s?
5. Payoff lands on emotion or a joke?
6. Ending: choice with options (or "parte 2" question) + "Seguinos para más", last picture echoes the first?
