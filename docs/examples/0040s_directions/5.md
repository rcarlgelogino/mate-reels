Sentence: "Pasa una vuelta, pasan dos, y se termina el termo." About 3.4 seconds.

What the viewer must understand without sound: time passes (the light turns orange, the string lights glow brighter), the suegro keeps circling, vos sinks lower and lower on his chair, and finally the termo is empty: the suegro turns it upside down and only one drop falls.

Why this picture: from straight above the round becomes a clock: the suegro walking around the table is the hand of the clock, and the light changing is the time passing.

The set: seen from above: the round table with the white cloth in the centre, the four chairs and seated people around it, the brick circle, the edge of the grass; the dog at the front right curled up asleep again. The light is now late afternoon: warm orange, long soft shadows, the bulbs of the string light glowing.

Movements.
At 0.0 the suegro walks a full circle around the table, fast, like in a time-lapse; at each person except vos he stops for a moment and the mate moves in and out of their hands.
Vos, as the suegro passes him, leans more and more: at the start he sits upright, at the end he hangs on the chair, head down, face sad.
At 0.75 the suegro stands at the back of the table, lifts the termo and turns it upside down; a single drop falls into the mate. He shakes the termo once and looks into it with one eye.

Camera. High above the table looking almost straight down (4.5 metres up, a little in front), then slowly lowering and tilting forward to 3.2 metres while turning about 40 degrees, so at the end we see the empty termo and vos slumped.

Timing: "Pasa una vuelta" = the first fast circle. "pasan dos" = the second. "y se termina el termo" = the upside-down termo and the single drop.

Sounds: fast footsteps, a ticking clock, a single "plip" of the drop.

Continuity: dusk light stays for the rest of the reel; the termo is empty.
