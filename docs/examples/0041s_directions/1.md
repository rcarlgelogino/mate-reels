## 1a — frame 0: the clogged mate and the man behind him
What the viewer must understand without sound, in the first half second: a young man sucks on a mate and nothing comes, and a big man with a moustache stands right behind him and watches. Problem and threat in one picture. The voice says "Episodio uno", so the picture must already be the story, not a title card.

Set: the family patio in the afternoon sun (about 16:00). Behind Tomás we see the brick floor circle, the edge of the round table with the white cloth in the lower frame, a medialuna plate out of focus, the green grass, the white back wall with bushes and the lemon tree on the right edge of the frame. The string lights hang above, not lit (daytime). The sun comes from the front left, so Tomás' face is lit on his right cheek (screen left) and the suegro behind him gets a hard rim on his boina.

Tomás sits on his low chair at the back of the table, facing the camera. Light blue shirt, beige trousers, short neat dark hair. In his right hand (screen left) the calabaza with the steel bombilla; the bombilla is in his mouth. Face "suck": eyes wide open, small pupils, brows high, lips pursed round, cheeks puffed and red. He holds still, only the cheeks shake a little. His left hand rests on his thigh.

Don Rubén stands behind Tomás' right shoulder (screen left, half a metre back): black boina, thick moustache, dark red polo, grey trousers, the green termo in his left hand at hip height. Face neutral, lids half down: he watches the bombilla. He does not move at all; that stillness is the threat.

Camera: in front of Tomás at face height, a little lower than his eyes, about 1.4 m away, framing both heads: Tomás in the lower middle, the suegro's head above and left. Very slow push-in (1.4 → 1.2 m). Sound: a long dry suck noise that gets nothing.

## 1b — the whole family stares
Reverse shot from behind and above Tomás' left shoulder: we see the table from his side, and all faces turned to him. Nélida (grey bun, round glasses, purple cardigan) on the right, chin on her hand, curious. Caro (dark ponytail, yellow T-shirt) on the left, a polite smile that freezes. Facu (curly hair, green football shirt) at the far left lifts his phone with his right hand at 30 % of the shot and points it at Tomás: a red REC light blinks on the back. Pancho, the dog in the front right corner, lifts his head at 50 %.

Tomás' back and the mate fill the lower left corner of the frame. Camera still, slight drift to the right. Sound: a single tick (a clock in the silence).

## 1c — his face, sweat
Close on Tomás' face from the front. Sweat drops start at 20 % and run down from his temple. His eyes go to the abuela (screen left, 25 %), to Caro (55 %), then up over his shoulder to the suegro (80 %). Face "worried": brows up in the middle, mouth wavy. The mate stays at his chin. Camera very slow push-in.
