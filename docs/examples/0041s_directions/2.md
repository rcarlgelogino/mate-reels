## 2a — "Si chupás más fuerte…"
The viewer must see the first option being tried: he sucks harder. The voice says "Si chupás más fuerte" and the picture does exactly that on the word "fuerte".

Same patio, same afternoon sun. Medium shot of Tomás from the front, a little to his right, so the suegro is out of frame for once: the threat is the empty bombilla now. Behind him the white wall, the bushes and the lemon tree, soft. The table edge and the cloth at the bottom.

Tomás: mate in the right hand at his mouth, bombilla between the lips. At the start he takes a breath (chest up), then on "fuerte" he bends forward about 15 degrees, cheeks puff up even more and go red, eyes bulge, brows jump to the top. Small shaking of the whole upper body (effort). The left hand grips his knee.

Camera: starts at 1.3 m and pushes in to 1.0 m on "fuerte"; a small camera shake for the effort. Sound: two suck noises, the second longer and higher, then nothing.

## 2b — "…no sale nada."
Nothing comes. He takes the bombilla out of his mouth and sits back, dizzy: crossed-out eyes become spirals ("dizzy" face), three small yellow stars circle above his head. The mate comes down to his chest, still in the right hand, still full and green (the clog stays: continuity for the next shots). His head wobbles once.

Camera from slightly more to the side (30 degrees), 1.5 m, at face height, still. Behind him now the suegro is back in the frame, out of focus, arms crossed around the termo. Sound: a little sparkle when the stars appear.

The gag: he sucks so hard he almost faints, for a mate that gives nothing.
