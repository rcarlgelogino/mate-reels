## 3a — "Pero si movés la bombilla…"
The second option: move the bombilla. Everyone in a ronda knows: you never touch the bombilla of someone else's mate; it is the cebador's work.

Extreme close on the mate in Tomás' right hand: the calabaza with its green yerba top and the steel bombilla, his ball hand around the body of the gourd. His left hand comes in slowly from the screen right edge, on "movés", and stops two fingers away from the bombilla. It shakes a little. The background is the white tablecloth and the brick floor, very soft.

Camera: at mate height, 0.7 m, still, the mate in the centre. Sound: a tick on "bombilla".

## 3b — the suegro's eyes
Push-in on Don Rubén's face, from his front, slightly below (low angle = power). His lids drop to half, the inner corners of the brows go down, the moustache goes straight: face "angry" at the start, "rage" from 60 % on. His head turns to Tomás' hand. The termo in his left hand, at hip height, does not move. Behind him the blue sky and the string lights.

Camera: 1.2 m → 0.8 m, slow, steady. Sound: a low buzz at 60 %.
