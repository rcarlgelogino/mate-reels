## 4a — "Y si le avisás…"
The third option: tell the cebador. Over the suegro's shoulder: in the foreground the back of his boina and his red shoulder (screen left), in the middle Tomás looking up at him. Tomás turns his head to the suegro, lifts his left index finger (hand up to shoulder height), opens his mouth: face "surprised", as if he is about to say "disculpe…". The mate stays in his right hand.

Camera: behind the suegro, 1.6 m from Tomás, at Tomás' eye height. Still. Sound: a short pop on "avisás".

## 4b — "…quedás como que su mate está mal cebado."
Don Rubén, proud: he leans back a little (chest out), lifts the green termo to chest height, face "proud": lids half down, a smirk under the moustache. He is the cebador of the house; a bad mate would be an insult to him. Low angle from the front, 1.4 m; behind him the sky. He nods twice slowly.

## 4c — Caro shakes her head
