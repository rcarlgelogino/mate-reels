## 6a — the freeze and the vote
On "¿Qué hacés?" the picture freezes on Tomás' face: a short white flash, then everything stands still (the sweat drops hang in the air). Face "scared": brows high, tiny pupils, mouth wavy; the clogged mate in his right hand at his chest; the suegro behind his shoulder, frozen too.

Under his face the two vote words punch in side by side: MUEVO (terracotta) o AVISO (cream). The face stays in the upper half; the words sit in the lower third, never over the face. They stay until the end while the voice says: "Comentá MUEVO o AVISO. El episodio dos sigue a la mayoría." Sound: a pop when the words appear.
