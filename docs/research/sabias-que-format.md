## The formula (planeta_curioso / loquepasahoy, 19–24 s)
1. **0–3.5 s, the normal action:** a toy figure does an everyday thing in its home (cut bread, cool soup, dry hair, pass the salt). Voice: "¿Sabías que para X lo recomendable es Y…"
2. **3.5–6 s, "…y no Z":** the absurd alternative, always a real named machine or thing with a model name (Eurofighter Typhoon, Chinook CH-47F, Dulcinea, the M-30 boring machine, an aircraft carrier catapult, caustic soda). The object enters the same set or is used in the everyday action (the jet engine held like a hair dryer).
3. **6–16 s, 3–4 real numbers**, each on its word, each with its own picture: weight, size, power, speed. Shown as small dark rounded pill labels on the object ("16 m", "23 t", "90 kN cada uno", "SECADOR 2.000 W"), yellow dimension lines, a dial gauge, a yellow counter box with a black border ("21.900 km/h", "0 → 265 km/h EN 2 SEGUNDOS").
4. **16–21 s, the consequence as a list of three losses:** "lo más probable es que te quedes sin sopa, sin mantel y sin comedor". Each loss is its own destruction beat (the bread board, then the counter, then the kitchen), and the last frame shows the burnt or wrecked figure with the three labels SIN PELO / SIN TOALLA / SIN BAÑO.
5. **21–23 s, CTA:** "Sígueme para más datos." over the wrecked figure.

## Pacing and craft
- Shots: about one picture every 1.5–2 s, plus bursts of very fast cuts (0.15–0.3 s) during escalation and destruction.
- Captions: two lines, white bold, upper third, the spoken word highlighted in yellow/orange as it is said.
- Labels are small, black, rounded, uppercase white text, attached to the thing they measure, popping in on the word.
- Look: blocky/voxel or vinyl-toy figures, bright flat daylight, sky blue, simple sets; one home set plus one "world of the machine" (airbase, tunnel, army field).
- Object handling: the absurd object is shown at true scale next to the house (the joke is the size difference), then used in the everyday action.
- Facts are real and specific (model names, kW, tonnes, km/h); the absurdity is only the combination.
- Sound (not measured here, the page was hidden): whoosh on every cut, impacts on labels, a big boom on the destruction.

## Other shapes seen
- **curios0n** (flat 2D, dark, crude humour, 55–64 s): a real question ("¿Por qué el gallo canta?"), an experiment or study shown (Japan, 24 h light), a graph, onomatopoeia stickers (¡ÑAC!, ¡PUM!), a running gag figure; slower continuous camera moves instead of cuts.
- **loquepasahoy "el último pueblo en pie"**: viewers comment their town; each day a catastrophe (tsunami, volcano, meteorite) removes towns on a map, with counters. Participation as the engine (like the vote serial).
