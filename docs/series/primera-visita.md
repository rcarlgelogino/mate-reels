# Series bible: "Primera visita" (serial with a vote)

One young man and his girlfriend's family. Every episode is one mate moment of his first visits, told in 20–30 s, and ends on a dilemma with two bad options. The viewers vote in the comments with one word; the next episode follows the majority and says so in its first line ("Ganó MUEVO: …").

## Rules for every episode
- Frame 0: the problem and the threat in one picture (the problem object close, the family watching).
- If → But → So: the first option fails, the second has a price, then the clock runs (3 s countdown).
- Density: a new picture every 1–2 s; reactions of every family member are separate quick shots.
- The last shot freezes on his face with the vote: two words in capitals, the same words in the caption ("Comentá MUEVO o AVISO").
- Next episode: first sentence names the winner and its consequence, then a new dilemma grows out of it.
- Facts about mate customs must be right; the dilemma is about a real rule of the ronda.

## Cast (fixed looks, all episodes)
- **Tomás (vos)**, 25: short neat dark hair, light blue shirt, beige trousers. Polite, nervous, wants to be accepted.
- **Don Rubén (el suegro)**, 58, the cebador: black boina, thick moustache, dark red polo, grey trousers, green termo (a red spare termo). Proud of his mate; silent judge.
- **Nélida (la abuela)**, 82: grey bun, round glasses, purple cardigan, grey skirt. Sits next to Tomás; secretly on his side.
- **Caro (la novia)**, 24: dark ponytail, yellow T-shirt, jeans. Signals with her face.
- **Facu (el cuñado)**, 30: dark curly hair, green football shirt. Always films with his phone; wants Tomás to fail.
- **Pancho (the dog)**: light brown, sleeps in the front right, lifts his head at every tension.

## Place
The family patio (3D world "patio"): round table with white cloth, yerba pack and medialunas; brick floor, lemon tree, parrilla with smoke, string lights. Seats: Tomás at the back facing the camera, Nélida to his right, Caro to his left, Facu at the left side. Don Rubén stands and serves, walking on an outer circle.

## Episodes
1. **El mate tapado** — the bombilla is clogged in his hand. MUEVO (move the bombilla: a sin in the ronda) or AVISO (tell the cebador: his mate is badly prepared).
