# build3d: new 3D style (model: @wemakebuild)

Reference: instagram.com/wemakebuild (123k followers, 24 reels, 0.4–30.5 M views each; top: "How a Wooden Plank Becomes a Bridge" 30.5 M, "Shopping Cart Becomes a Roller Coaster" 7.1 M, "Cardboard Box Becomes an Airplane" 2.1 M). The user wants this look, pace and structure; the figures and places are our own mate versions (no copied characters, logos or scenes).

## What their reels do
- **Format:** "How [everyday thing] becomes [big thing]" — one object is improved step by step. Every step: a problem, a funny failure, a fix with a technical name, then the next problem.
- **Length:** 90–140 s (much longer than our 35–45 s).
- **Pace:** a cut or a camera move every 1–2 s; drone fly-overs, fast push-ins on faces, POV shots, a fixed cross-section shot that comes back (ground cut open, layers of soil).
- **Look:** semi-realistic sunny 3D: striped green lawn, olive trees, real clouds, small houses, soft shadows. Objects are realistic (wood, metal, water).
- **Figures:** cartoon people with big heads, big eyes, big noses, moustaches, arms and legs, very exaggerated faces (shock, pride, panic).
- **Text:** one word at a time in the middle of the screen (white, bold, dark outline); technical names as big yellow labels ("TRUSSES", "LIFT HILL"); failures as red stamps ("CRACK!", "REJECTED", "PUSH #3").
- **Sound:** many sound effects, light music.
